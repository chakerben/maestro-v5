# French

Reference of `maestro-pm:04-writing`, loaded when the text is in French.

- Vouvoiement by default with clients unless they tutoie first; tutoiement
  in internal notes. No anglicisms where a common French word exists in the
  team's usage ("livrer", not "shipper", in client text).
- Accents kept in ALL CAPS and in file names shown to clients.
- Typography: non-breaking space before `; ? !` and `:` and inside
  « guillemets »; "e.g." becomes "par ex.".
